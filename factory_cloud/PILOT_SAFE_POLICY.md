# PILOT_SAFE — pre-customer certification authority

A real customer is not a QA environment.

No Factory may use a real customer, proprietary customer data, a live payment, a production incident, or a customer-visible failure as evidence that the product is safe enough to pilot.

## Required order

THESIS/SCOPE → END-TO-END PRODUCT → WEB COMPLETENESS → ECONOMIC/EVIDENCE INTEGRITY → SECURITY/ADVERSARIAL → OPERATIONAL CONTINUITY → SUPPORT CONTINUITY → SYNTHETIC COMPANY CERTIFICATION → REAL-WORLD SHADOW VALIDATION → PILOT_SAFE → CONTROLLED REAL PILOT → PILOT_VALIDATED → COMMERCIAL_READY → MARKET_VALIDATED.

## PS0–PS9

- PS0: thesis, buyer, wedge, economic/entitlement basis and non-claims are frozen enough to build.
- PS1: the complete customer/service outcome path exists end to end.
- PS2: web UI is complete across normal, loading, empty, error, auth, recovery, permission and responsive states.
- PS3: calculations are deterministic/reproducible and every finding has evidence lineage; UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
- PS4: tenant isolation and adversarial boundaries are proven.
- PS5: durable recovery, backup/restore and observability are proven.
- PS6: support intake, severity, ownership, escalation, customer communication and resolution continuity are proven.
- PS7: one or more synthetic companies execute the service A→Z, including billing sandbox and deliberate failure/recovery paths.
- PS8: realistic/public/reconstructed evidence is processed in shadow mode without exposing a real customer to product risk.
- PS9: source truth, exact source HEAD, assisted API, delivery economics and onboarding/exit criteria are explicit and current.

Every gate is fail-closed. Missing, stale, symbolic, inherited or merely asserted evidence is UNKNOWN and blocks certification.

## Scheduling authority

Before PILOT_SAFE, Factory Cloud prioritizes: safety/regressions → PILOT_SAFE blockers → shared capability work that unblocks multiple Factories → web/end-to-end completeness → support/observability/backup → evidence/calculation integrity → synthetic/shadow proof. Commercial exposure is downstream of PILOT_SAFE.

This policy grants no merge, deploy, production, customer-data, payment or market-validation authority.
