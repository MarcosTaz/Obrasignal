# ObraSignal — Europe Frontier / Balkans + Caucasus cell

Status: research in progress. This file is intentionally isolated from canonical market scope and the master execution queue until country evidence passes the cell gates.

## Invariants

- National depth must add material value beyond TED/eForms where TED applies.
- Prefer an existing shared family before any bespoke connector.
- Technical access is not evidence of commercial reuse rights.
- No CAPTCHA/login/private API bypass.
- Native language and publisher taxonomy/identifiers are preserved.
- `SUPPORTED`, `READY`, `LIVE` and `PRODUCTION` require repository/runtime evidence, not research intent.

## Repository baseline — 2026-08-25

- Switzerland is already a canonical BUILD market with `CH_SIMAP`, a ready opt-in SIMAP connector and a merged implementation PR. It is therefore a SKIP-WITH-EVIDENCE market for this cell unless new evidence reveals a material missing national signal.
- Ukraine is already canonical HOLD. The blocker is commercial/operational execution risk, not absence of technically interesting procurement data. This cell may research Prozorro depth but must not silently promote Ukraine.
- Turkey, Serbia, Georgia, Albania, North Macedonia, Moldova, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Montenegro are not present in the current 135-market canonical scope registry. Research may propose a future portfolio rebase, but this cell will not mutate canonical counts/order as a side effect.
- Shared-family fitness currently distinguishes TED Search, publisher eForms XML and three materially different OCDS transports. A Balkan/Caucasus family will be created only after two publishers prove the same transport/completeness contract.

## Switzerland — completion record

Classification: EXISTING DEDICATED CONNECTOR / SKIP WITH EVIDENCE.

The merged SIMAP implementation already proves official public project-search, publisher-native construction filtering, lot/project identity, correction and revocation/abandonment lifecycle, explicit legal release timing, bounded pagination, incremental tracked-target refresh, opt-in runtime wiring and offline deterministic CI. The source registry deliberately claims only `TENDER` plus search/incremental/detail/lots; awards/contracts are not overclaimed. No new Swiss connector is justified by this cell.

## Research discipline for remaining countries

Each country must close: primary official source, access, reuse rights, freshness, historical depth, plan/forecast availability, tender/award/contract lifecycle, construction discriminator, identity chain, fragmentation, TED overlap, competitor pressure, 100-hour economics and shared-family fit. Unknown fields remain UNKNOWN; they are not inferred into GREEN gates.
