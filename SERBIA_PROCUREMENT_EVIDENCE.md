# Serbia — Public Procurement Portal evidence gate

Status: RESEARCH / NO PRODUCTION CLAIM.

## Repository facts

Serbia has no canonical market-scope entry, source-registry entry or connector on current main. The existing shared-family layer can only be reused after the official Serbian publisher contract is proven to match one of its pagination/completeness transports.

## Priority hypothesis to verify

Serbia is a high-priority Balkan candidate if the official Public Procurement Portal exposes machine-readable procurement plans plus tender/award/contract lifecycle with stable procedure identifiers. That combination would create value beyond EU/TED-style notice discovery and could justify either an existing OCDS family or the first member of a future Balkan family.

## Hard gates

- official machine route and pagination;
- procurement-plan availability/freshness;
- works/construction discriminator;
- tender/award/contract identity chain;
- historical depth;
- commercial reuse/redistribution rights;
- national coverage versus local/sector fragmentation;
- second compatible Balkan publisher before creating a new family.

Until verified, classification: **TECH LAB CANDIDATE / HOLD FOR SOURCE CONTRACT**.
