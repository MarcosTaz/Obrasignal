# ObraSignal GTM — Cohort 2 priority ranking — 2026-08-29

## Ranking purpose

Prioritize a small, evidence-rich experiment rather than sending volume blindly. Ranking uses only observed workflow evidence, relevance to the proposed construction/technical-installations wedge, and quality of public contact path. It does not score willingness to buy.

## Tier A — strongest first test set

### 1. Electro Planície, Lda.

Why first:
- official site describes electrical infrastructure, telecom and renewable-energy construction/maintenance;
- official site explicitly references public and private clients;
- current public-procurement evidence confirms a 2026 public electrical contract;
- official public contact path is available.

Unknown: whether tender discovery/triage is internally painful, who owns that workflow, and whether a shortlist improves a real decision.

### 2. Electroinstal

Why high priority:
- official site shows electrical + AVAC execution and a substantial project portfolio;
- public-procurement evidence confirms a 2026 AVAC public contract;
- official site describes a commercial installation/sales department and publishes a direct company contact route;
- operational similarity to the original AVAC/electrical wedge is high.

Unknown: tender sourcing process and responsible decision maker.

### 3. Electro Instaladora de Carnaxide — Estudos, Projectos e Montagens Eléctricas, Lda.

Why high priority:
- current public-contract evidence plus a much longer observed public-contract history than a one-off award;
- direct alignment with electrical public works;
- public phone/business route exists.

Constraint: email route still lacks first-party/authoritative verification, so it should not be used yet.

### 4. Instaléctrica, Lda.

Why high priority:
- public-procurement data indicates multiple public contracts, not just one isolated award;
- Portuguese civil-protection authority register publishes business contact details;
- direct electrical-installation fit.

Constraint: first-party site/contact was not established; role ownership remains unknown.

### 5. H2Plus Energy, Lda.

Why high priority:
- public electrical-work contract evidence;
- official site and public contact route;
- electrical engineering/project work fits the workflow hypothesis.

Unknown: frequency of public-tender discovery and whether this is a meaningful acquisition channel for the company.

## Tier B — useful comparison set

- Fiel Técnica, Lda. — official contact + AVAC public contract, but smaller observed contract signal in this pass.
- CANAS — Engenharia e Construção, S.A. — strong public-work signal and official contact, but materially larger/broader organization may have a more formal incumbent tender process and therefore higher replacement/adoption friction.
- Beneproject — Construções, Lda. — relevant AVAC/public-work signal, but contact evidence is not yet first-party.
- Omninstal — Electricidade, S.A. — very strong infrastructure/public-work relevance and official contact, but enterprise scale likely means mature internal processes; valuable as a contrast hypothesis rather than the first small-company test.

## Tier C — hold until contact verified

- GT Renewables, Lda. — procurement-workflow evidence exists, but no first-party or authoritative company contact was established in the current pass.

## Recommended experiment shape

Do not contact all ten at once. If/when outbound communication is explicitly authorized, begin with a bounded 3–5 company Tier A wave, preserve each response, then decide whether to change ICP, message, offer or channel before expanding.

This ranking deliberately values learning quality over outreach count.
