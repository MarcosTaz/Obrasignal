## Evidence rules

- `NO_RESPONSE` means no reply observed by the measurement cutoff; it does not prove non-delivery or rejection.
- `WILL_TEST` is not a pilot outcome.
- a meeting is not a pilot.
- a free pilot is not willingness to pay.
- a quoted price is not payment.
- payment is not retention.
- customer-reported workflow facts must be marked as customer-reported unless independently observed.
- record explicit negative evidence verbatim enough to preserve meaning, but avoid unnecessary personal data.

## Wave decision rule

After a bounded Tier A wave, do not automatically increase volume. First classify whether weak performance is most consistent with:
- wrong ICP/problem;
- wrong recipient;
- unclear proposition;
- poor timing/channel;
- weak proof;
- offer/pricing friction;
- insufficient sample to conclude.

Change one meaningful variable at a time where practical and preserve the previous result.
