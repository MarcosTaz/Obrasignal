# ObraSignal — MicroWorks + Services + Continuous Opportunity Hunting

Status: official strategic architecture. This document does not activate automated hunting, outreach, quoting or acceptance.

## North-star outcome

ObraSignal should treat idle or underutilized contractor capacity as economic capital and answer:

> Given the real capacity window that exists, what is the best legitimate use of that capacity without harming core projects, strategic bids or higher-value work?

MicroWorks is not a gig marketplace and not an infinite classified feed. It is a capacity-recovery intelligence loop.

## Integrated loop

OFFICE capacity event → DEAD TIME / capacity-gap economics → AUTO-HUNT policy → source-family incremental discovery → deduplicated opportunity object → MicroWorks/service classification → MOBILIZE economics → SHADOW conflict/opportunity-cost check → TrustBridge proportional requester confidence → COMMAND best-use decision → ACTION prepare/contact/quote inside authority → OFFICE actuals → Contractor DNA / Outcome Graph / relationship learning.

A valid result can be `DO NOTHING` when available work destroys margin, creates fatigue, conflicts with core work or carries excessive risk.

## Capacity Unit

Use one reusable abstraction for sellable or redeployable capacity. A `CAPACITY UNIT` may represent:
- worker;
- crew;
- trade/specialty;
- technician/service capability;
- equipment/tool;
- vehicle;
- subcontract capacity.

The abstraction must carry only decision-relevant facts such as location/base, availability window, skills/certifications, usable hours, constraints, economic cost, equipment dependency and authorization/privacy boundaries. It must not become a generic HR or fleet system.

## MicroWorks

MicroWorks are defined primarily by capacity fit and duration/mobilization profile, not price alone. Candidate work can include small repairs, maintenance, emergency response, technical assistance, small subcontract packages, inspections, measurements, CAD/BIM/estimating/documentation services and other commercially sellable technical capacity.

Every candidate must be evaluated against:
- duration and exact availability window;
- crew/equipment/service fit;
- location and route;
- mobilization cost and time;
- installed economic margin/contribution;
- overtime/fatigue/safety;
- conflict with core projects;
- strategic opportunity cost;
- requester/payment confidence;
- relationship/expansion potential, kept separate from direct profit.

Priority hierarchy by default:
`CORE PROJECT > STRATEGIC BID > HIGH-VALUE SUBCONTRACT > MICROWORK`.

MicroWorks may outrank normal idle time only when the complete economics justify it.

## Services

The opportunity model must not assume that value requires a physical construction package. Sellable services may include surveying/measurement, CAD/BIM, estimating, project/document preparation, inspection, certification, technical visits, equipment operation, transport, assembly, maintenance and specialist technical support.

Services and physical MicroWorks should share the same capacity/economic decision contracts whenever possible.

## Continuous Opportunity Hunting

Principle: **CAPACITY CREATES SEARCH.**

A hunting request may be generated by OFFICE/SHADOW events such as:
- project finishing early;
- crew/equipment becoming free;
- cancellation or absence;
- schedule delay that temporarily releases capacity;
- predicted utilization gap.

The engine operates only inside durable user policies. Example policy fields:
- specialties/capacity types;
- crew-size bounds;
- duration bounds;
- normal and conditional economic radius;
- minimum contribution/margin;
- mobilization ceiling;
- working-time/safety constraints;
- protected projects and no-interference rules;
- autonomy/contact limits.

The user should not have to repeat the same hunt every day once policy is configured.

## Source families and access discipline

Do not invent one global MicroWorks source. Maintain source families with technical access separated from commercial reuse rights.

1. `OFFICIAL PROCUREMENT`: official APIs/open data/RSS/XML/JSON/CSV/OCDS, including small procurement and maintenance opportunities.
2. `LOCAL PUBLIC OPPORTUNITIES`: municipalities, hospitals, schools, universities, utilities, housing/facility bodies and legitimate local procurement systems.
3. `COMMERCIAL / PRIVATE`: only public API, license, partnership or authorized integration. Unsupported automation is `PARTNERSHIP REQUIRED`.
4. `OBRASIGNAL NETWORK`: progressively sourced demand from primes, contractors, facility/property managers, suppliers and clients through authorized network flows.

Never use CAPTCHA bypass, private API reverse engineering, auth circumvention, prohibited scraping or rate-limit bypass.

## Frequency and incremental ingestion

Cadence follows signal half-life rather than a universal polling interval:
- urgent/rescue/MicroWorks: push/webhook when supported; otherwise source-safe minutes-level cadence;
- normal tenders/awards/changes: typically 15–60 minutes where technically justified;
- plans/forecasts/future demand: daily/weekly by source;
- regulation/forced demand: daily or event-triggered;
- supplier/travel/local-service checks: on-demand for a concrete opportunity;
- OFFICE capacity: event-driven/near-real-time.

Connectors must prefer `watermark / last_seen_id / last_modified / cursor / version / checksum` incremental contracts. The operational question is **what appeared or changed since the last successful observation?** Full reloads require a justified recovery/backfill path.

## Canonical opportunity identity, freshness and lifecycle

Small opportunities often duplicate quickly. Deduplication should preserve all evidence while presenting one opportunity object. Candidate evidence includes source ID, requester/entity, description, location, dates, value, contact and bounded semantic similarity.

Every opportunity should support:
- `discovered_at`;
- `source_updated_at` when publisher provides it;
- `verified_at` when independently/live verified;
- deadline/window;
- freshness/confidence;
- lifecycle such as open/filled/expired/cancelled/stale/no-response/duplicate.

Urgent opportunities require stricter freshness because stale work has almost zero value.

## Economic matching

The core question is not “can we perform it?” but:

> Is this the best use of this capacity during this exact window?

Compare candidate contribution with:
- IDLE COST;
- mobilization;
- travel time;
- direct/indirect labor;
- material/equipment;
- fatigue/overtime;
- schedule conflict;
- foregone alternative work;
- relationship/expansion option value (separate, evidence-backed).

MOBILIZE is mandatory for MicroWorks. A small job with attractive gross revenue can still be rejected after travel/mobilization.

## Minimum Economic Price and Rapid Quote

For simple, sufficiently understood work, compute an explainable `MINIMUM ECONOMIC PRICE` from labor, material, travel/mobilization, equipment, overhead, risk and policy margin. It is contractor-specific decision support, not competitor-coordinated pricing and must not use confidential competitor data.

`RAPID QUOTE` may later convert a simple request into cost → price → proposal with minimal administration. Safety/structural uncertainty or missing scope must force clarification/site inspection rather than false precision.

## Urgent / Rescue

Urgency may increase willingness to pay but also increases overtime, material, safety and execution risk. Emergency repair candidates must explicitly model call-out cost, availability and risk. Rescue Mode can share this source/matching infrastructure after its own gate.

## Route intelligence and batching

When economically useful, MicroWorks matching may consider:
- current project location;
- company base;
- next project;
- legitimate equipment base/location;
- en-route windows.

`MICROWORK BATCHING` may group multiple nearby tasks in one day only when sequencing, travel, commitment risk and schedule are credible. The UI should show a small recommended combination, not a route-planning product.

## Reverse Market and network

When authorized and sufficiently trusted, ObraSignal may publish minimal verified capacity availability to relevant buyers. Reveal only what is needed: capability, broad location/radius, time window and commercial constraints. Do not disclose sensitive workforce/project information.

This is a future network capability, not an initial marketplace. It should depend on verified capacity, TrustBridge, Action authorization and sufficient network density.

## Requester confidence

TrustBridge should be proportional to transaction size/risk. Small jobs still need protection from fake requests, poor scope and non-payment, but verification must not create disproportionate friction. Keep facts, evidence and probabilistic confidence separate; never promise payment/performance.

## Photo / AI triage

Future triage may infer likely specialty, urgency, materials, crew and duration from customer-authorized photos/video/text. It must not replace professional inspection where structural, electrical, gas, fire, safety or other high-risk conditions demand competent assessment.

## UX

Do not create a MicroWorks app inside ObraSignal. Surface contextual `CAPACITY OPPORTUNITIES` only when capacity gaps exist.

Desired decision compression:

- candidate #1: expected contribution, distance, duration, fit/confidence;
- candidate #2: same;
- candidate #3: same;
- recommended combination if materially superior;
- explicit `DO NOTHING` when that is economically best.

Notify only for high fit, short deadline, genuine capacity gap, material expected contribution or strategic relationship. Weak candidates should be grouped or suppressed.

## Learning and metrics

Track measured vs estimated separately:
- relevance / false positives;
- response and fill rate;
- realized contribution/margin;
- idle capacity recovered;
- travel/mobilization cost;
- quote/admin time saved;
- repeat business;
- relationship expansion;
- predicted vs actual duration/cost.

North-star metric candidate: `IDLE CAPACITY RECOVERY VALUE` — economic value created or idle cost avoided from capacity that otherwise would have remained unused. Never double-count avoided idle cost and earned contribution in external ROI claims.

## Promotion gates

### Capacity Unit / Services contract
Must represent crew, technician/service and equipment capacity without becoming HR/fleet/ERP bloat and with proportional privacy.

### Source validation
Prove opportunity volume, lawful access/reuse, freshness, stable incremental ingestion and lifecycle/filled detection in at least one commercially relevant launch market.

### Economic matching
Prove that MOBILIZE + DEAD TIME + SHADOW can reject bad small jobs and rank a small number of better uses. Opportunity cost must be explicit.

### Continuous Hunting
Prove policy-driven event triggers, notification discipline, source-safe cadence and low false-positive/noise rate.

### Pilot / Rapid Quote / Action
Prove users respond, realized economics remain positive after mobilization, administration falls materially, and no core project is harmed. Acceptance/contractual commitment remains inside explicit Action authority.

No stage advances because many opportunities were found; it advances only when capacity is genuinely recovered at useful economics.

## Anti-bloat

Do not build a generic gig marketplace, classified feed, travel/routing product, CRM, workforce marketplace, HR suite or directory. ObraSignal owns the capacity/economic model, evidence, matching, orchestration and decision compression; commodity execution should use integrations/partners where better.

Principle: **COMPLEXITY IN THE ENGINE. SIMPLICITY IN THE DECISION.**
