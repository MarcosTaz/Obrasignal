# DGC territorial-scope delta — 2026-08-28

## Truth state

- Portuguese Law 41/2004 Article 13-B(2) describes the DGC corporate opposition list as a **national-scope list**.
- Article 13-B(5) states that entities promoting direct-marketing communications must consult the monthly updated list made available by the DGC on request.
- Article 13-C separately provides for cross-border enforcement cooperation.
- These provisions do **not**, by themselves, prove that a US-incorporated legal person contacted at a generic corporate mailbox is outside the consultation duty.
- Therefore: `CROSS_BORDER_US_APPLICABILITY = UNRESOLVED`, not PASS.

## Operational action

Gmail draft `r4293171257938926382` to `dgc@dgconsumidor.gov.pt` was updated to request both:

1. the current Article 13-B corporate opposition list; and
2. official clarification whether the list-consultation requirement applies where a Portugal-based sender contacts exclusively a US-incorporated legal person through the company's generic corporate mailbox.

State: **NOT SENT / OWNER_ONLY**.
