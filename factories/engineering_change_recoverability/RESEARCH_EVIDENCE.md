# Forensic research evidence — 2026-08-28

This file preserves the public evidence used to pass T0 narrowly. It does not prove willingness to pay or customer recovery outcomes.

## Materiality and frequency

- Springer, *AI-artifacts in engineering change management – a systematic literature review* (2024): cites estimated EUR 20k–50k cost per change and an average of ~1,000 changes/month at a German automotive OEM; also describes EC as common and economically material.
  - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00163-023-00430-6

## Contractual/commercial mechanism

Public supply agreements show that customer-requested engineering changes can trigger cost estimates, price/schedule adjustments, and liability for materials or obsolete inventory, subject to the governing terms and mitigation.

- SEC exhibit: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1759546/000110465922058868/tm2215185d1_ex10-1.htm
- SEC exhibit: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1391984/000119312507114089/dex1028.htm

These are examples, not universal legal rules. Every real entitlement remains contract-specific.
