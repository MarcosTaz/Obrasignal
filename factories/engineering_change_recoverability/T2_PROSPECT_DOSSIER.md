## Selection rule

Rank targets by observable proof that engineering changes already create a cross-functional commercial job involving cost, price, tooling, margin, claims/recovery, open items and P&L. This is a prospecting score, not business validation and not evidence that any company currently loses recoverable value.

Weights:
- 25 — explicit engineering-change commercial/recovery ownership
- 20 — explicit program P&L / profitability accountability
- 15 — open-item / cost / quote / payment reconciliation burden
- 15 — engineering-to-finance cross-functional handoffs
- 15 — visible tooling/material/obsolescence/capacity economic effects
- 10 — buyer-role accessibility from current public evidence

## Qualification questions

A target is qualified only if at least four are true:
- >20 customer-driven engineering changes per quarter on a program/account or an equivalent material change cadence;
- engineering-change quotes pass through Engineering + Program + Sales + Finance/Costing;
- tooling/NRE/material/obsolete inventory/revalidation costs are material;
- customer approval/PO/price update can lag technical implementation;
- commercial open items or bump/price-history trackers exist;
- notice/prior-approval/quotation deadlines matter;
- recovery is reconciled separately from quoted or approved value;
- buyer owns program/account margin or P&L.

## Kill conditions

Deprioritize a target if:
- engineering changes are rare or immaterial;
- one system already reconciles revision, causal cost, contract basis, notice, approval, invoice and payment with independently verified completeness;
- customer contracts categorically make the supplier bear the relevant changes;
- no usable evidence trail exists;
- buyer cannot act on findings or has no economic ownership.
