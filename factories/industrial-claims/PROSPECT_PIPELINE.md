## Discovery qualification questions
1. After a customer/OEM changes scope, drawing, validation, timing or launch requirements, where are incremental engineering/NRE/tooling/freight costs tracked?
2. How often do those costs remain in spreadsheets/email instead of becoming an approved change/charge?
3. Who owns the recovery process: program, commercial, finance, claims or plant management?
4. Can you identify one closed/recent program where customer-directed change created material incremental cost?
5. What documents normally exist: PO/contract, ECN/change order, emails, time ledger, supplier invoices, freight, credit notes?
6. How do you check prior payments/credits and avoid double-claiming?
7. What makes a case die: missing authority, late notice, weak causation, poor cost allocation, customer rejection?
8. Would a bounded independent rapid scan of one historical case be useful if it returns either a defensible dossier or a documented NO CLAIM?
