## Beachhead
Start commercial validation with Portuguese automotive/component suppliers before widening geography. This is a sequencing choice, not a product-scope expansion.

Public AFIA evidence checked on 2026-09-04 supports a dense export-oriented automotive-components ecosystem in Portugal. The working wedge remains customer/OEM-driven engineering/change/NRE recovery.

## Fit model
Prioritize an account only when public or customer-supplied metadata supports at least four of six conditions:
1. supplier relationship to OEM/Tier 1 customers;
2. engineered-to-program, tooling, validation, launch or change-intensive work;
3. multiple handoffs across engineering/program/commercial/finance;
4. economics where one missed change/NRE position can materially exceed the scan fee;
5. international customer exposure and formal purchasing/change processes;
6. identifiable senior economic owner or program/commercial owner.

Do not infer a claim from company size, customer relationship or complexity.

## Stop / pivot rules
- If buyers consistently report no material customer-change/NRE reconciliation gap, re-test the ICP before widening scope.
- If evidence is systematically unavailable, test earlier capture within the same mechanism rather than jumping categories.
- If legal representation is the primary need, route to customer counsel/specialist rather than presenting VINDORUM as legal counsel.
- Adjacent costs enter scope only when repeatedly evidenced inside the same customer/program/change chain.
