# CERTACTUM — SUPPLIER RECOVERY GTM TARGET ACCOUNT THESIS

Status: research/qualification thesis only. No listed organization is represented as having leakage, a valid claim, willingness to buy, or recoverable value. Those facts require direct discovery and evidence.

## Beachhead
Start commercial validation with Portuguese automotive/component suppliers before widening geography.

Reasons:
- dense supplier ecosystem and direct access to an industry association/member universe;
- meaningful Tier 1/Tier 2 export activity and customer/OEM dependence;
- engineering, tooling, launch, industrialization and program-change complexity is common enough to test the NRE/change mechanism;
- one successful bounded case can validate both the commercial workflow and the evidence architecture before cross-border expansion.

This is a go-to-market sequencing decision, not a product-scope expansion. Product scope stays customer/OEM-caused engineering/change/NRE recovery.

## Account fit model
Prioritize an account only when public/discovery evidence suggests at least four of these six conditions:
1. supplier relationship to OEM/Tier 1 customers;
2. engineered-to-program, tooling, validation, launch or change-intensive work;
3. multiple handoffs across engineering/program/commercial/finance;
4. material per-program economics where one missed NRE/change recovery can exceed a diagnostic fee;
5. international/export customer exposure and formal purchasing/change processes;
6. identifiable senior economic owner or program/commercial owner.

Do not infer a claim from company size, customer relationship or complexity alone.

## Roles to map per account
Primary economic buyers:
- CFO / Finance Director;
- Commercial Director / Sales Director with recovery ownership;
- Program Director / Program Management Director;
- Head of Controlling / Plant Controller;
- Head of Engineering / Industrialization where NRE ownership sits there.

Secondary champions:
- Key Account Director for strategic OEM/Tier 1 customers;
- Tooling/Launch/Industrialization Manager;
- Claims/Recovery lead where such a role exists.

Avoid starting with generic procurement unless discovery confirms procurement owns supplier-side customer recovery. This product is not procurement-side vendor recovery.

## First-contact thesis
Lead with a process question, not an accusation and not a recovery promise:

> When a customer-driven engineering or program change creates incremental NRE, how do you currently reconcile what was documented, what the commercial basis supports, what was claimed, what was approved, and what was actually settled?

The objective of the first call is metadata qualification only.

## Qualification before enrichment
Do not spend contact-enrichment/search credits on an account until public research supports supplier/program-change fit. Then use the Certactum metadata triage to decide whether a specific buyer/contact is worth enriching.

Minimum pre-enrichment account evidence:
- supplier/program relationship is credible;
- engineered/change-intensive work is plausible;
- likely economic owner roles exist;
- no obvious scope conflict with Supplier Recovery.

## Commercial experiment 1
Cohort: 10 accounts maximum, Portugal automotive components.
Target: 1–2 senior roles/account only after account qualification.
Primary CTA: 20-minute metadata-only recovery process review.
No confidential documents requested.
No recovery number promised.
No success fee proposed before the mechanism and baseline are understood.

Experiment success is not opens/clicks. Record separately:
- qualified conversations;
- confirmed workflow pain;
- confirmed existence of a bounded historical case;
- willingness to use a secure evidence path;
- willingness to pay for a bounded scan/audit;
- paid diagnostic/audit;
- verified outcome.

## Stop / pivot rules
- If senior buyers consistently report that customer-change NRE is already fully reconciled with no material leakage, do not widen the Factory immediately. Re-test ICP/subsegment and case timing first.
- If pain exists but documents are systematically unavailable, test whether the value proposition is earlier change-capture/reconciliation within the same mechanism; do not jump to unrelated claims categories.
- If buyers demand legal representation as the primary service, partner/escalate rather than positioning Certactum as legal counsel.
- If a different adjacent cost category repeatedly appears inside the same program/change chain, record it as adjacency evidence; automate only after repeated real cases.
