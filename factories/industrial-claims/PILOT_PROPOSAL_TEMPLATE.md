# CAUSLEDGER — SINGLE-CASE RECOVERY AUDIT PROPOSAL TEMPLATE

> Commercial template for assisted industrial recovery work. Adapt to the customer and obtain appropriate legal/accounting review where required. CausLedger does not provide legal representation unless separately and lawfully arranged.

## 1. Objective
Review one bounded supplier-side customer-caused engineering/change/NRE case and determine whether the available documentary chain supports:
- a defensible commercial recovery candidate;
- further document requests/review; or
- a NO CLAIM / insufficient-evidence conclusion.

No recovery amount or customer liability is guaranteed.

Customer / OEM: `[customer]`
Program / project: `[program]`
Change / event: `[event]`
Case family: customer-directed engineering/change cost (NRE)
Review period: `[dates]`

## 3. Customer-provided inputs
Minimum agreed sample:
- relevant contract / PO / commercial terms or excerpt;
- ECN / change order / instruction / correspondence;
- engineering/NRE cost evidence;
- prior credit/payment/settlement evidence;
- notice/deadline evidence if available.

Customer confirms it is authorized to provide the agreed materials for this review.

## 4. CausLedger work
- documentary index and evidence traceability;
- contract/PO/change truth extraction for commercial review;
- event chronology;
- causation assessment including competing causes;
- customer-responsibility basis assessment without asserting a legal conclusion;
- gross-cost reconstruction;
- mitigation / prior-recovery reconciliation;
- currency and calculation lineage;
- notice/deadline risk identification;
- missing-evidence and attack list;
- audit-grade assisted recovery dossier;
- one review meeting.

## 5. Deliverable
One current-revision CausLedger Assisted Recovery Dossier with disposition:
- NO CLAIM / rejected;
- NEEDS REVIEW / documents;
- documented candidate for independent review;
- documented final outcome if the engagement later includes outcome tracking.

The dossier explicitly distinguishes observed evidence, human assessment and unresolved interpretation.

## 6. Exclusions
Unless added in writing, scope excludes:
- legal opinion or representation;
- tax advice;
- accounting audit/opinion;
- automatic external claim submission;
- negotiation with the end customer;
- full mailbox/ERP discovery;
- unrelated claims/programs;
- currency conversion;
- guarantee of payment or recovery.

## 9. Delivery target
Target after receipt of the complete agreed sample: rapid-scan disposition within 2 business days; bounded forensic dossier target within 5 business days. These are operating targets, not guarantees, and pause when required evidence/customer clarification is outstanding.

## 10. Data handling
- use only the agreed document set and purpose;
- use a customer-specific authorized transfer channel;
- preserve tenant/case separation;
- do not place real documents in an unverified public/external runtime;
- raw source contents are not persisted by the Industrial service;
- retain/delete temporary working copies according to the agreed rule;
- notify the customer of a material data/evidence integrity incident affecting its case.

## 11. Customer responsibilities
The customer remains responsible for:
- source-document accuracy and authority to share;
- business/legal decisions;
- external notice/claim/negotiation authority;
- identifying known prior payments, credits and settlements;
- final approval of any external action.
