**VINDORUM** is the only company and product brand for this Factory.

The descriptive category **Industrial Supplier Claims & Margin Recovery** may be used to explain the market, but it is not a second brand.

## Customer-facing rule
Every customer-facing surface, dossier, workflow, commercial asset, sender identity and deployment must identify the product as **VINDORUM**.

The internal repository path `industrial_claims` may remain as a technical implementation detail where renaming it would add migration risk without customer value.

## Isolation
VINDORUM must never reuse another project's domain, mailbox, product identity, database namespace or deployment identity.

## Name meaning
