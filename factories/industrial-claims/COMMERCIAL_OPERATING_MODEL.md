# CERTACTUM — COMMERCIAL OPERATING MODEL

## Direction
Certactum is an assisted supplier-side recovery business that is automating itself. Software is a control/evidence layer; the first product is a defensible recovery dossier plus a reconciled recovery ledger.

## Frozen initial wedge
Until real commercial proof exists, the critical path is **supplier-side customer/OEM-caused cost and margin recovery**.

### Primary case type to close first
**Customer-directed engineering/change cost (NRE / change-driven incremental cost).**

A case is in-scope when there is a documentary chain linking:
1. customer / contract / PO;
2. customer-directed or customer-caused change/event;
3. incremental engineering, validation, rework, launch, tooling-support or other NRE cost;
4. a contractual/documentary basis for customer responsibility or reimbursement review;
5. cost source and calculation;
6. prior recovery/credit/payment check;
7. notice/deadline truth;
8. human review.

### Direct adjacencies only after the primary case type is operational
- premium freight / expedite directly caused by the same customer change or disruption;
- dedicated investment/tooling exposure directly bound to the same program/change;
- customer-caused production disruption and rework;
- contractually supported volume-shortfall exposure on the same customer/program relationship;
- clearly supported material/cost adjustments caused by the same commercial event.

Do not expand into warranty supplier recovery, procurement-side vendor recovery, generic rebate recovery, general pricing optimization, M&A recovery or unrelated claims software. Those are separate economic mechanisms and are outside this Factory unless a case is explicitly part of the same customer-caused industrial-change chain.

## Economic truth model
Every case must preserve these distinctions:

`DOCUMENTED COST → MITIGATION → NET DOCUMENTED EXPOSURE → EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED AMOUNT → CLAIMED → APPROVED → SETTLED`

The chain is monotonic and fail-closed. Software must never infer that documented cost is money owed.

The recovery reconciliation must separately expose:
- unsupported exposure — cost exists but documentary/commercial support is incomplete;
- supported-unclaimed — evidence-supported value has not yet entered a claim/action workflow;
- claimed-unapproved — claim exists but is not approved/settled truth;
- approved-unsettled — approved value has not yet been economically realized;
- supported-unsettled — total evidence-supported value still not realized.

## Assisted-delivery default
Early delivery uses **software + human review + evidence traceability + professional dossier + reconciliation ledger**. Manual work is acceptable when controlled, recorded and reviewable. Repeated manual work becomes automation backlog only after it occurs in real or adversarial delivery.

## Commercial deliverable
The paid deliverable answers:

DOCUMENTS → CONTRACT/PO/CHANGE TRUTH → EVENT → CAUSATION → CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY BASIS → COST → MITIGATION → EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED AMOUNT → CLAIM/APPROVAL/SETTLEMENT RECONCILIATION → PRIOR RECOVERY CHECK → EVIDENCE → CONFIDENCE → NOTICE/DEADLINE → NEXT ACTION.

If the chain is incomplete, the output is **NO CLAIM / NEEDS REVIEW / EVIDENCE GAP**, never an inflated recovery number.

## Positioning
Certactum is not an ERP/PLM replacement and is not an OEM warranty adjudication system. It occupies the evidence-and-recovery layer between customer-driven engineering/commercial events and the supplier's finance/commercial outcome.

The differentiating control point is:

**customer change/event → causation → incremental cost → commercial basis → evidence → supported recovery amount → claim state → settlement truth → independently reviewable dossier.**

Competitive products may start from supply-chain planning signals, warranty claims, pricing/rebate agreements or pre-approval engineering-change exposure. Certactum stays narrow around supplier-side customer-caused change/NRE recovery until real commercial evidence proves an expansion.

## Initial offer ladder — pricing hypotheses, not validated WTP
1. **Recovery Triage** — 30–45 minute fit review using non-confidential metadata only. `€0` while building qualified pipeline; no document intake and no recovery promise.
2. **Rapid Recovery Scan** — bounded assisted review of one case/sample after secure intake is permitted. Initial hypothesis: `€1,500–€3,500` fixed.
3. **Forensic Recovery Audit** — reconstruct one material case and produce a Certactum evidence-indexed dossier + recovery reconciliation. Initial hypothesis: `€5,000–€15,000` fixed depending on document volume/complexity.
4. **Recovery Support** — only with explicit customer authority after dossier approval. Initial hypothesis: `10–20%` of independently verified incremental recovery, with prior-recovery baseline and no double counting.
5. **Continuous Leakage Monitoring** — only after repeated case patterns justify automation. Initial hypothesis: `€1,500–€5,000/month` per business unit/program portfolio plus bounded onboarding.

Pricing remains a hypothesis until real costly buyer behavior validates it. Discounting may change price, never truth standards.

## ICP
Prioritize industrial suppliers where a single missed change/NRE recovery can materially exceed the audit fee:
- automotive Tier 1 / Tier 2 suppliers with OEM-driven engineering changes;
- electronics, cable/harness, precision machining, plastics, tooling and engineered components;
- aerospace/industrial-equipment suppliers only where the same customer-change/NRE mechanism is present;
- organizations with program management + engineering + finance/commercial handoffs and fragmented evidence across email, PO/change documents, ERP exports and cost ledgers.

High-fit buying roles: CFO/Finance Director, Commercial Director, Program Director, Head of Controlling, Head of Engineering/Program Management, Claims/Recovery lead where one exists.

## Initial discovery questions
A qualified opportunity should answer, without leading the buyer:
1. How are customer-driven engineering/change costs identified today?
2. Where do PO/change approval, engineering hours, tooling/rework and prior credits live?
3. What amount was documented, what was claimed, what was approved and what was actually settled?
4. Which recovery opportunities were written off because evidence was incomplete or assembled too late?
5. Who owns the decision to submit or negotiate a claim?
6. Which notice/deadline or customer-process requirements can kill a recovery?
7. Can a bounded historical case be reviewed without sending confidential documents through ordinary email?

## Commercial proof gates
- `T0` — product and synthetic/adversarial truth only.
- `T1` — qualified buyer confirms the problem and current workflow.
- `T2` — buyer provides a bounded real case or secure sample under an authorized data path.
- `T3` — paid scan/audit or equivalent costly commercial commitment.
- `T4` — independently verified incremental recovery or defensible true-null outcome delivered.
- `T5` — customer payment received and repeat/monitoring intent observed.

No email open, generic interest, demo attendance or synthetic result counts as paid validation.

## Execution priorities
Every task must materially help detect, prove, reconcile, deliver, recover or monetize a real case. Deprioritize cosmetic dashboards, broad taxonomies, optional integrations, new verticals and infrastructure not required for paid assisted delivery.

## Progress metrics
Primary metrics are:
- qualified recovery opportunities;
- cases processed;
- evidence gaps closed;
- supported-unclaimed value identified;
- approved-unsettled value identified;
- dossiers produced/delivered;
- document samples securely received;
- pilots;
- paid audits;
- verified incremental recovery value;
- customer payments;
- delivery hours/cost per case;
- repeat engagements;
- repeated manual steps automated.

Commits, tests and screens are supporting evidence, not the business outcome.

## Commercial truth
- Pricing remains a hypothesis until tested externally.
- No claim is money owed merely because cost exists.
- No candidate is represented as legally recoverable without the required documentary/human/legal basis.
- No real recovery, payment, customer or willingness-to-pay evidence may be fabricated.
- Assisted commercial presentation and metadata-only triage can start before confidential intake is production-ready.
- Real confidential documentary intake remains blocked until production identity/access/storage/data-handling controls and the explicit readiness gate are genuinely green.
