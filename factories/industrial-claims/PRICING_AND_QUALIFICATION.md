# CAUSLEDGER — PRICING & QUALIFICATION HYPOTHESIS

## Commercial principle
Sell a bounded, evidence-first assisted recovery service before selling software. Pricing is a hypothesis until prospects accept/reject it.

## Initial ICP
Industrial suppliers with:
- direct OEM / Tier-1 / major-customer contracts or POs;
- engineering changes, tooling/NRE, launch/rework or premium-freight exposure;
- enough transaction value that one missed recovery can justify an audit;
- finance/commercial/program ownership able to provide documents;
- fragmented evidence across email, ERP, spreadsheets and shared drives;
- no requirement to replace existing ERP/PLM/contract systems.

Best early buyer roles: CFO/Finance Director, Commercial Director, Program Director, Sales Director, Claims/Contract Manager, Managing Director of a supplier business unit.

## Disqualifiers
- no documentary customer/event link;
- expectation that CausLedger invents a legal entitlement;
- request to inflate claim value;
- inability/unwillingness to provide a bounded evidence sample;
- purely consumer claims or unrelated verticals;
- tiny economic exposure relative to review cost.

## Offer ladder — test, do not assume

### 1. Rapid Recovery Scan
**Hypothesis:** free to €750 for a bounded sample, depending on access/friction.

Deliverable: candidate/no-claim disposition, missing-evidence list, initial net-potential reconstruction if supported, notice/deadline risks, and recommendation whether a forensic audit is justified.

### 2. Single-Case Forensic Recovery Audit
**Hypothesis:** €2,500–€7,500 fixed fee for one bounded customer-caused cost/NRE case.

Deliverable: audit-grade dossier with evidence index, causation/responsibility review, calculation, prior-recovery check, deadline truth, attack list and next action.

### 3. Portfolio Audit
**Hypothesis:** €7,500–€25,000+ depending on programs, customers, documents and case count.

### 4. Success component
**Hypothesis:** 10–20% of incremental documented recovery attributable to the engagement, only where legally/commercially appropriate and agreed in writing. Never count a success fee twice and never bill it against unsupported “potential”.

### 5. Monitoring
Only after repeated case value is proven. Hypothesis: €1,000–€5,000+/month depending on programs and monitoring scope.

## Pilot commercial test
Preferred first paid path:
1. bounded sample;
2. rapid scan;
3. if defensible value exists, quote a fixed-fee single-case audit;
4. optional success component;
5. deliver dossier;
6. record delivery hours, customer feedback, outcome and payment evidence.

## Minimum economics to track per case
- quoted fee;
- cash collected;
- analyst hours;
- reviewer hours;
- specialist/legal hours if any;
- software/processing variable cost;
- acquisition effort;
- documented recovery outcome;
- success fee if triggered;
- contribution margin.

## Pricing experiments
Do not change all variables simultaneously. Test in this order:
1. free vs paid rapid scan;
2. €2.5k vs €5k vs €7.5k bounded single-case audit based on complexity/value;
3. fixed-only vs fixed + success component;
4. willingness to expand from one case to portfolio/monitoring.

A rejected price is evidence. Record objection type: no pain, no authority, no documents, low exposure, budget, trust, timing, incumbent process, or price.
