# T3 Gate — COMMERCIAL PROOF

Status: `NOT REACHED`.

## What does not count
- email sent;
- email delivered;
- email opened;
- reply;
- meeting;
- generic interest;
- willingness to see a demo;
- willingness to share non-sensitive example data;
- verbal statement that the problem exists.

## What can count
At least one independently observable costly commercial behavior:
1. paid diagnostic;
2. paid pilot;
3. signed commercial commitment with a credible budget/procurement path;
4. equivalent strong WTP evidence that creates a real economic or organizational commitment.

## Required supporting truth
Before promoting to T3, record:
- customer/legal entity;
- buyer/owner function;
- commercial artifact or payment evidence;
- exact scope;
- price/commitment;
- whether real data access is authorized;
- measurement boundary;
- known incumbent tooling/consultants;
- unresolved assumptions.

Interest alone never changes `REAL_CUSTOMERS`, `PAID_PILOTS`, `REVENUE_RECEIVED` or `VERIFIED_RECOVERY`.
