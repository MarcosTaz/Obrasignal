DEBITPROOF / PROVISIONAL WORKING NAME

Validate warranty debits before financial acceptance.

A bounded pre-acceptance control for automotive suppliers. It does not assume customer debits are wrong and does not replace ERP, QMS, OEM portals or specialist negotiation support.

Assisted pilot

100–500 debit lines, 1–3 programs, applicable approved contract/warranty versions, reviewed root-cause evidence where available, and prior credit/payment evidence.

Truth boundary

Potential saving ≠ disputable value ≠ submitted dispute ≠ approved credit ≠ received credit ≠ cash. Commercial proof requires real costly customer behavior; synthetic recovery never counts.
