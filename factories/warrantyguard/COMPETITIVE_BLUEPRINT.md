# WarrantyGuard / DebitProof — Best-in-Class Competitive Blueprint

## Strategic position
Do not become a generic OEM warranty administration suite. Become the strongest **supplier-side customer debit truth, defense and recovery control layer** sitting beside OEM portals, ERP and existing warranty systems.

## Best capabilities observed in market leaders
- SKYLYZE: multi-OEM data adapters, automatic claim checking, centralized analytics, VDA KPI dashboards, part-analysis workflows, intelligent text classification, trend detection and warranty-cost forecasting.
- Tavant: supplier contract catalog, OEM/supplier part mapping, supplier portal, bulk CSV processing, automated recovery claims, KPI reporting, audit and AI-enabled anomaly/quality workflows.
- Syncron: end-to-end claims workflow, manage-by-exception, multi-level approvals, supplier recovery, returns, supplier performance insights, ERP/service integration and configurable interfaces.

## WarrantyGuard differentiation
1. Pre-acceptance debit defense: debit is never treated as liability merely because it arrived from an OEM/customer.
2. UNKNOWN as a first-class state with money suppression.
3. Contract + document + clause + evidence + reviewer provenance on every material decision.
4. Exact separation of draft dispute, submitted dispute, approved credit, received credit and cash.
5. Deadline radar that refuses unsupported business-day calculations.
6. Decision journal and segregation-of-duties for high-value external approvals.
7. Trend intelligence explicitly SIGNAL_ONLY — never automatic root cause or liability.
8. Forecasts explicitly scenario-only — never silently promoted to accounting provisions.
9. OEM Adapter Registry with version/hash lineage and unknown-field detection.
10. Executive Command Center joining Finance, Warranty, Quality and Commercial truth in one view.

## Product rule
A competitor feature is adopted only when it improves one or more of: money protected, evidence quality, human decision speed, deadline control, auditability, interoperability, operational scalability, recovery truth, or buyer confidence. Feature count alone is not a success metric.
