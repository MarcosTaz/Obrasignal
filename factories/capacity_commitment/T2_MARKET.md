## Narrow commercial offer

### Working offer name

**Capacity Commitment Recovery Audit**

### One-line outcome

For automotive suppliers that invested against a customer program or volume commitment, reconstruct the gap between commitment and actual consumption, separate forecast from enforceable/commercially supportable commitment, quantify unrecovered dedicated investment after mitigation/prior recovery, and produce an evidence-backed action package.

### What it is not

- not generic claims consulting;
- not ERP/S&OP/forecasting software;
- not legal advice;
- not automated debt collection;
- not a promise that economic exposure is recoverable;
- not a claim that an identified amount has been recovered or paid.

### Starting ICP

Automotive Tier-1/Tier-2 suppliers, initially roughly **€20M–€1B revenue**, with at least one of:

- customer-specific tooling, welding/assembly cells, hot-stamping lines, injection assets or NRE;
- nomination/minimum-volume/flex-window/volume-linked amortization basis;
- meaningful customer concentration;
- repeated program changes, delays, cancellations or release reductions;
- ERP/EDI/call-off/forecast/shipment records available;
- CFO/CEO/Commercial/Program authority accessible.

### Primary buyer / champion

Economic buyer, in priority order:

1. CFO / Finance Director / VP Finance;
2. CEO / Managing Director in mid-market suppliers;
3. Chief Commercial Officer / Commercial Director / Business Development Director.

Likely operational champions/users:

- Commercial / Key Account;
- Program Management;
- Controlling / FP&A;
- Finance;
- Sales Operations;
- Plant / Industrial management when the exposure is asset-specific.

### Trigger

Contact becomes high-priority when there is public or buyer-confirmed evidence of:

- OEM production restructuring or plant/model uncertainty;
- lower-than-planned customer volumes;
- program delay/cancellation/phase-down;
- customer-specific capacity investment;
- new dedicated tooling/line launch with volume dependency;
- sales decline caused by automotive demand slowdown;
- major customer concentration plus current OEM volatility;
- disputed close-out or unamortized tooling/NRE/capacity.

## Minimum pilot dataset

The pilot intentionally starts with one customer + one program and a bounded period.

Required or explicitly marked missing:

1. nomination/LTA/PO/award letter or equivalent commercial anchor;
2. relevant amendments/change notices;
3. commitment/minimum/flex-window basis;
4. forecast/release/call-off history for context;
5. actual shipped/consumed units for the same program/period/unit/currency;
6. dedicated capex/tooling/NRE/labor/material cost basis;
7. amortization basis and current amortized amount;
8. mitigation/reuse/resale evidence;
9. prior customer credits/payments/recoveries;
10. notice/claim deadline basis and absolute date;
11. evidence references sufficient for human review.

## Deliverable

A controlled assisted pilot returns:

- executive exposure/recoverability summary;
- commitment vs actual baseline;
- shortfall and dedicated-capacity/investment exposure;
- mitigation and prior-recovery reconciliation;
- contract/amendment/customer/program integrity state;
- recoverability state with explicit reasons;
- evidence and missing-evidence register;
- notice/deadline state;
- claim/action package;
- explicit human-review requirement before external submission.

Correct zero recovery is a successful result.

## Delivery hypotheses — not yet market-proven

These are commercial hypotheses for T2/T3 testing, not established facts.

### Primary pilot hypothesis

**€3,500 paid pilot** for one customer + one program + one bounded period, with assisted manual review and the T1 package as the output.

### Alternative hybrid hypothesis

**€1,500 setup + 7.5% of incremental cash actually received** that is attributable to the supported recovery package, with no success fee on theoretical exposure or an unaccepted claim.

### Later monitoring hypothesis

Recurring monitoring becomes relevant only after a real pilot proves that the same commitment/actual/recovery logic recurs. Do not sell recurring monitoring before that proof.

### Commercial guardrails

- do not promise recovery;
- do not quote theoretical exposure as recovered cash;
- do not use a success fee as evidence of PMF;
- do not lower pricing automatically after silence;
- distinguish free eligibility/discovery from a paid pilot;
- success fee, if tested, is based on real cash received, not claim value.
