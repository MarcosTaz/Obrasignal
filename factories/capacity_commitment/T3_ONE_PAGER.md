# Capacity Commitment Recovery Audit

## Automotive supplier program-volume & dedicated-investment recoverability

Automotive suppliers often commit tooling, cells, automation, NRE/ED&D, material, labour or other customer-specific capacity against an awarded or nominated programme. When actual releases, timing or programme scope later diverge from the relevant commercial basis, the remaining economic exposure can become difficult to reconstruct across contracts, amendments, releases, shipments, amortisation, mitigation and prior customer payments.

**Capacity Commitment Recovery Audit** is a bounded, evidence-first assisted review of one customer + one programme + one defined period.

It does **not** assume that a shortfall is recoverable and does not manufacture claims. A correct conclusion of **£0 / €0 recoverable** is a valid outcome.

## When it fits

A programme is worth screening when several of these are present:

- customer-specific tooling, assembly/manufacturing capacity or automation;
- nominated/minimum/flex-window/volume-linked amortisation basis;
- actual releases or shipped volumes materially below the relevant commercial anchor;
- delay, cancellation, phase-down, sourcing change or end-of-programme close-out;
- unrecovered tooling, dedicated capex, NRE, committed material or labour exposure;
- amendments, credits or prior commercial settlements that make the true net position unclear;
- notice/claim timing that must be preserved.

Forecast-only demand is not treated as a firm customer commitment.

## What we reconstruct

For the bounded programme we reconcile:

1. **Commercial anchor** — nomination, LTA, PO, award letter, minimum/flex basis or other support;
2. **Amendments** — later terms or changes that may supersede the original basis;
3. **Actual consumption** — releases/call-offs and shipped/consumed units for the same programme, period, unit and currency;
4. **Dedicated investment** — tooling, capex, NRE/ED&D, labour, material or other customer-specific exposure;
5. **Amortisation** — what has already been absorbed through production;
6. **Mitigation/reuse** — assets, material or capacity that can be reused, resold or otherwise mitigated;
7. **Prior recovery** — credits, payments or settlements already received;
8. **Timing/evidence** — notice windows, missing evidence and the support required for any external action.

## Deliverable

The pilot produces a reviewable action package containing:

- commitment-vs-actual baseline;
- unrecovered economic exposure calculation;
- amortisation reconciliation;
- mitigation and prior-recovery reconciliation;
- contract/amendment/customer/program integrity state;
- recoverability state with explicit reasons;
- evidence and missing-evidence register;
- notice/deadline state;
- assisted commercial action package;
- one review call.

Every positive result remains subject to human review before external submission or negotiation.

## Minimum data for one programme

Only the bounded case is requested initially:

- nomination/LTA/PO/award or equivalent commercial anchor;
- relevant amendments/change notices;
- commitment/minimum/flex/amortisation basis;
- forecast/release/call-off history for context;
- actual shipped/consumed units;
- dedicated tooling/capex/NRE/labour/material cost basis;
- amortisation history;
- mitigation/reuse evidence;
- prior credits/payments/recoveries;
- notice/deadline terms;
- existing claim/correspondence history if applicable.

Unrelated customer or corporate data is not required.

## Commercial model

### Fixed assisted pilot

**£3,000 / €3,500** for one customer + one programme + one bounded period, subject to final scope.

### Hybrid alternative

For a qualified case where fixed-fee risk is the main objection:

**£1,250 / €1,500 setup + 7.5% of incremental cash actually received** and contractually attributable to the supported recovery action.

No success fee is charged on theoretical exposure, submitted claim value, disputed amounts or approved-but-unpaid amounts.

## What this is not

- not legal representation;
- not litigation;
- not a guarantee of recovery;
- not automated debt collection;
- not a replacement for ERP/SAP/CLM/S&OP or internal commercial teams;
- not a broad data-migration project;
- not a claim that an identified economic exposure has been recovered or paid.

## First step

A **15-minute eligibility screen** around one current or recent programme.

The core question is simple:

> Was there a supportable customer/program volume or amortisation basis, did actual releases materially diverge, and does supplier-specific investment remain unrecovered after mitigation and prior payments?

If the answer is no, the process stops. If the answer is potentially yes, the next step is the bounded data request and paid pilot scope.
