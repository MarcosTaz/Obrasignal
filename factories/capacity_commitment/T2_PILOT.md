# Capacity Commitment — Paid Pilot Offer

Status: **T2 COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESIS / NOT YET MARKET-PROVEN**

## Offer

**Capacity Commitment Recovery Audit**

A controlled assisted audit for one automotive customer + one program + one bounded period.

We reconstruct:

`COMMERCIAL COMMITMENT -> ACTUAL VOLUME -> DEDICATED INVESTMENT -> MITIGATION / PRIOR RECOVERY -> CONTRACT / EVIDENCE / DEADLINE -> RECOVERABILITY STATE -> ACTION PACKAGE`

The audit does not promise recovery and does not convert economic exposure into a legal/commercial claim without evidence.

## 15-minute eligibility screen

A program is worth screening when all three questions are plausibly yes:

1. Did the supplier invest in customer/program-specific tooling, capacity, NRE, material, labor/headcount or facilities?
2. Did actual releases/consumption materially underperform a nomination, minimum, flex-window, amortization or other supportable commercial volume basis?
3. Is some dedicated investment still unrecovered or disputed after reuse/mitigation and prior customer credits/payments?

If commitment is only forecast, evidence is missing, the investment was already fully amortized/paid, mitigation eliminates exposure, the contract basis fails, or the deadline is expired, a **€0 claimable result is a correct outcome**.

## Pilot scope

One:

- customer;
- program;
- commercial commitment family;
- bounded period;
- currency/unit basis;
- exposure calculation;
- recoverability review;
- action package.

No ERP migration. No multi-program transformation project.

## Minimum customer input

Required or explicitly classified missing:

1. nomination, LTA, PO, award letter or equivalent anchor;
2. relevant amendments / program-change notices;
3. minimum, firm, flex-window or amortization-volume basis;
4. forecast / release / call-off history for context;
5. actual shipments / consumed units for the same customer, program, period, unit and currency;
6. dedicated tooling / capex / NRE / labor / material cost basis;
7. amortization basis and current recovered/amortized amount;
8. reuse, resale, redeployment or other mitigation evidence;
9. prior customer credits, settlements, payments or recoveries;
10. notice / claim-window basis and absolute deadline;
11. source references sufficient for human review.

Start with an anonymized eligibility outline if confidentiality prevents immediate document transfer.

## Turnaround hypothesis

Target operational hypothesis:

- eligibility finding: within **2 business days after a complete bounded input pack**;
- assisted action package: within **5 business days after required evidence is complete enough for the evaluated state**.

These are **T2 hypotheses**, not proven SLAs. Missing/contradictory documents pause the relevant conclusion rather than being guessed around.

## Stop / fail-closed conditions

Do not present a positive claimable amount when any material state is unresolved, including:

- forecast-only commitment;
- wrong/unknown customer, program, period, unit or currency;
- incomplete/deduplicated/cancelled-order actuals not verified;
- amortization basis not verified;
- asset dedication not verified;
- mitigation not reviewed;
- prior recovery/payment not reviewed;
- wrong/stale contract;
- amendment not reconciled;
- evidence insufficient;
- notice window not verified;
- deadline expired;
- duplicate claim;
- already-paid or fully amortized cost;
- zero net exposure after mitigation/prior recovery.
