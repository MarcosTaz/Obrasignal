# Capacity Commitment — T3 Pilot Unit Economics

Status: **HYPOTHESIS / MARKET-TEST GUARDRAIL — NOT WTP PROOF**

Purpose: prevent the first paid pilot from becoming an unbounded consulting project whose delivery cost exceeds the commercial price.

## External benchmark signals — not customer willingness-to-pay

Current UK public market signals checked 2026-08-29:

- advertised UK management-consultant contract/interim median: approximately **£550/day** in the six months to 21 August 2026 (IT Jobs Watch figures compiled by ConsultingDemand; contractor pay, not consultancy billing);
- a July 2026 UK Senior Forensic Delay Analyst / Claims Specialist contract role advertised **£600–£750/day** (Randstad; contractor compensation, not consultancy billing);
- UK public procurement consultancy frameworks explicitly support fixed, time-and-materials and risk/reward pricing models, but do not prove a Capacity Commitment buyer price.

These are cost/market context only. They do **not** prove that an automotive supplier will pay £3,000, £3,500 or any other amount for this product.

## Current price hypothesis

### Fixed pilot

- UK: **£3,000**
- Euro markets: **€3,500**
- Scope: one supplier + one customer + one programme + one bounded period.

The price remains an **ASSUMPTION** until a real buyer accepts and pays it.

## Delivery-effort guardrail

The commercial scope says 5–10 business days as an elapsed delivery window. It must **not** mean 5–10 full analyst-days.

For the first three pilots, target:

- normal active founder/analyst effort: **≤ 3.0 working days**;
- stretch review threshold: **3.5 working days**;
- if evidence volume/complexity implies materially more work, narrow the period/data or propose a separately priced extension before continuing.

Do not absorb multi-programme or historical portfolio work into the one-program pilot.

## Shadow-cost model

Until real operating cost exists, use a planning-only shadow labour cost of **£600/day** for UK work.

At £3,000 fixed fee:

| Active analyst effort | Shadow labour | Contribution before other costs | Contribution % |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2.0 days | £1,200 | £1,800 | 60% |
| 2.5 days | £1,500 | £1,500 | 50% |
| 3.0 days | £1,800 | £1,200 | 40% |
| 3.5 days | £2,100 | £900 | 30% |
| 5.0 days | £3,000 | £0 | 0% |

This is an internal planning model, not an accounting statement and not a claim about founder salary, gross margin or market price.

## Why the tool matters economically

The T1 engine, persistence, red-team checks and report export exist to compress the repeated work:

- commitment/actual arithmetic;
- amortisation reconciliation;
- mitigation/prior-recovery deductions;
- fail-closed integrity classification;
- repeatable package/report generation;
- case reopening and audit trail.

If a pilot still requires five or more full analyst-days after a complete bounded dataset arrives, the current £3,000 hypothesis is economically weak and one of the following must change:

1. scope becomes smaller;
2. workflow becomes more automated;
3. price rises;
4. target segment moves to larger recoverable exposures;
5. the offer is rejected as insufficiently scalable.

## Buyer-value threshold hypothesis

Do not impose a fake minimum entitlement before evidence exists. For prospecting and prioritisation only, prefer cases where plausible supplier-specific economic exposure is **material relative to the pilot fee**.

Working prioritisation hypothesis:

- `< £25k` plausible exposure: lower priority unless repeat monitoring/portfolio value is strong;
- `£25k–£50k`: case-dependent;
- `> £50k`: stronger fixed-pilot economics if commercial support exists;
- `> £100k`: high-priority screen, still subject to all evidence/contract/mitigation gates.

This is not an entitlement threshold and must never be used to inflate a case value.

## Hybrid economics

Current hypothesis:

- **£1,250 / €1,500 setup**;
- **7.5% of incremental cash actually received** and attributable to the supported recovery action.

Use hybrid only when:

- the case is buyer-qualified;
- expected recoverable cash is material;
- attribution can be defined;
- setup fee covers bounded intake/reconstruction risk;
- an appropriate agreement is executed;
- legal/regulatory implications are reviewed where applicable.

Do not model the success fee on claim value or theoretical exposure.

## Measurement on every paid pilot

Track actual:

- fee contracted;
- fee received;
- active analyst hours;
- elapsed delivery days;
- number of source documents/rows;
- missing-evidence cycles;
- engine/report rework;
- external professional cost, if any;
- buyer outcome;
- our cash contribution after direct external cost;
- repeat/monitoring opportunity.

After **3 paid pilots**, replace the shadow model with observed effort and contribution data before fixing a long-term price.
