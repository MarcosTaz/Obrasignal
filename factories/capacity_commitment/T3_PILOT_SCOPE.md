# Capacity Commitment — Paid Pilot Scope Template

Status: **COMMERCIAL TEMPLATE — NOT A SIGNED AGREEMENT**

Use only after a real buyer case has passed the eligibility gate. Tailor customer/program/period/currency before external use. This document does not authorize legal representation or bind either party unless separately executed through an appropriate agreement/process.

## 1. Pilot objective

Determine, for one identified customer programme and bounded period, whether actual customer releases/consumption diverged from a supportable commercial or amortisation basis and whether supplier-specific tooling, dedicated capacity, NRE, material, labour or related investment remains economically unrecovered after amortisation, mitigation and prior customer recovery.

A correct outcome of **£0 / €0 recoverable** is a valid pilot result.

## 2. Scope boundary

Pilot covers exactly:

- Supplier: `[SUPPLIER LEGAL ENTITY]`
- Customer: `[CUSTOMER / BUSINESS UNIT]`
- Programme: `[PROGRAMME ID / NAME]`
- Period: `[START]` to `[END]`
- Currency: `[CURRENCY]`
- Unit: `[UNIT OF MEASURE]`

Anything outside this boundary requires a separate scope decision.

## 3. Included work

- commercial-anchor identification and evidence register;
- amendment/supersession reconciliation;
- firm/flex/forecast distinction;
- release/call-off vs shipped/consumed actual reconstruction;
- tooling/dedicated-capex/NRE/material/labour exposure reconciliation where supported;
- amortisation calculation;
- mitigation/reuse reconciliation;
- prior payment/credit/settlement reconciliation;
- notice/deadline state;
- recoverability classification with explicit reasons and unknowns;
- missing-evidence register;
- assisted commercial action package;
- one findings review call.

## 4. Explicit exclusions

Unless separately agreed and lawfully authorized, the pilot does not include:

- legal advice or legal representation;
- litigation or dispute representation;
- signing or submitting a claim in the customer’s name;
- settlement authority;
- customer negotiation authority;
- guarantee of recovery or payment;
- ERP/SAP/CLM implementation or data migration;
- broad historical multi-programme audit;
- automated external submission;
- production-system integration;
- collection of unrelated customer/corporate data.

## 5. Customer inputs

Customer supplies the minimum bounded dataset described in `T3_INTAKE.md`, including commercial anchor, amendments, actuals, investment cost basis, amortisation, mitigation/reuse, prior recovery and notice/action history.

Missing or ambiguous evidence may result in `UNKNOWN`, `NON_RECOVERABLE`, `ZERO_EXPOSURE`, `NOTICE_OR_LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, or a request for one specific missing item. Missing evidence will never be silently converted into a positive entitlement.

## 6. Deliverables

1. Executive summary.
2. Commitment-vs-actual table.
3. Economic exposure and amortisation reconciliation.
4. Mitigation/prior-recovery reconciliation.
5. Contract/amendment/customer/programme integrity state.
6. Recoverability classification with reason codes.
7. Evidence and missing-evidence register.
8. Notice/deadline state.
9. Assisted action/recovery package for human review.
10. Findings review call.

Every positive result remains subject to customer/human review before external submission, negotiation or reliance as legal advice.

## 7. Fixed-fee commercial hypothesis

Default pilot price:

**£3,000 / €3,500 fixed** for the bounded scope above.

This price is an active market hypothesis until accepted and paid by a real buyer.

Suggested payment structure for first pilots:

- 50% at pilot start / data acceptance;
- 50% on delivery of the bounded findings package.

`INVOICE_ISSUED != PAYMENT_RECEIVED`.

Do not mark T3 paid proof until actual payment is evidenced.

## 8. Hybrid alternative

Use only when:

- the case is buyer-qualified;
- fixed-fee risk is the principal objection;
- attribution of incremental recovery can be defined;
- economics support delivery risk;
- an appropriate agreement is executed.

Working hypothesis:

**£1,250 / €1,500 setup + 7.5% of incremental cash actually received and attributable to the supported recovery action.**

No success percentage on:

- theoretical exposure;
- submitted claim value;
- disputed amount;
- approved-but-unpaid amount;
- amounts already fully in process before the engagement unless attribution is explicitly agreed.

## 9. Timing hypothesis

Target assisted delivery for a complete bounded dataset: **5–10 business days**.

This is a service hypothesis, not a guaranteed SLA, until proven on real cases. The clock pauses for material missing/contradictory data or customer decisions.

## 10. Acceptance / completion

Pilot delivery is complete when the bounded findings package has been delivered and the review call offered/completed, even when the outcome is zero or unknown recoverability.

Commercial recovery from the OEM/customer is not a condition of fixed-fee pilot completion.

## 11. Truth and evidence rules

- forecast-only demand is not promoted to firm commitment;
- customer/programme/period/unit/currency mismatches fail closed;
- amendments can supersede original terms;
- shared/reusable assets reduce or block dedicated exposure as applicable;
- prior payments and mitigation are deducted;
- duplicate recovery is prohibited;
- expired/unknown notice windows are surfaced;
- legal interpretation is escalated for human/legal review;
- positive recoverability is not the same as money received.

## 12. T3 evidence handling

For Factory truth, distinguish:

- `PILOT_PROPOSED` — scope/price transmitted;
- `PILOT_ACCEPTED_UNPAID` — buyer accepted but no payment evidence;
- `AGREEMENT_SIGNED` — signed commercial commitment exists;
- `INVOICE_ISSUED` — invoice exists;
- `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` — real money received;
- `PAID_PILOT` — paid commercial proof;
- `DATA_PROVIDED` — meaningful proprietary case data supplied in a credible buying process.

Never collapse these states.
