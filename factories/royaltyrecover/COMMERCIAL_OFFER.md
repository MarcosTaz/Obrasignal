# RoyaltyRecover — Assisted Commercial Offer v0.2

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: PILOT OFFER HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET MARKET-VALIDATED
Initial beachhead: university/research TTO patent and technology running-royalty portfolios

## Entry offer

### Portfolio Royalty Recoverability Baseline

Question answered:

**Across the portfolio, which licensees deserve deeper investigation now, which appear correct on supported inputs, what evidence is missing, and where could a contractual audit/lookback window require attention?**

This is deliberately not sold as a formal contractual audit, legal claim service, or "AI found money."

The core differentiation test is not workflow novelty. Traditional royalty specialists already perform risk selection, desk reviews and full audits. RoyaltyRecover must make the **first portfolio-wide evidence baseline** materially cheaper and more reproducible than purchasing desk-review effort license-by-license.

## Minimum client inputs

Required for a calculable agreement:
- operative license agreement;
- amendments / side letters known to affect economics;
- royalty statements for tested periods;
- payment history / payment evidence;
- available sales, usage or transaction exports needed for the tested royalty base.

If a required source is unavailable, the output records the gap rather than inventing a value. Arithmetic may exist while recoverability remains blocked when the operative contract set is incomplete.

## Deliverables

Per agreement/licensee:
1. operative-version status and immutable document lineage;
2. human-reviewed sourced royalty rule(s);
3. sourced audit-right/lookback/notice fields where present;
4. expected-vs-reported-vs-paid deterministic reconciliation where possible;
5. supported variance, or UNKNOWN / NEEDS_DATA / PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW / NO_DEFENSIBLE_RECOVERY;
6. missing-evidence request;
7. deadline state;
8. transparent triage rationale;
9. formal-auditor requirement if the contract states one;
10. reviewer-controlled next operational step.

Portfolio output:
- tenant-scoped recoverability register;
- evidence completeness register;
- closing-window queue;
- no-current-recovery list (important for avoiding wasteful audit work);
- recommended desk-review/formal-audit shortlist;
- immutable formal-auditor handoff packets for reviewer-routed candidates.

## Escalation offer

### Audit Preparation & Outcome Reconciliation

Where a case survives the baseline:
- normalize calculation schedules;
- produce sourced questions and information requests;
- prepare evidence and calculation lineage for auditor/counsel;
- generate a formal-audit handoff packet after reviewer routing;
- track external audit findings and responses without rewriting them;
- reconcile settlement/payment evidence;
- record recovered cash as `UNVERIFIED` until payment evidence supports it.

When the agreement requires an independent CPA/qualified auditor, the formal audit is performed by that qualified professional, not relabeled RoyaltyRecover work.

## Public economic benchmark

The University of California's proposed royalty-audit identification approach reports approximately:
- **US$3,000 average desk-audit cost per license**;
- about **50%** of desk audits identifying concerns sufficient to move to a full audit;
- **US$40,000–US$60,000** average full-audit cost.

Source:
- https://regents.universityofcalifornia.edu/regmeet/july23/s1attach1.pdf

This benchmark does not imply every license should receive a desk audit. It supplies a reference for the economic waste RoyaltyRecover's portfolio triage must avoid.

## Pricing architecture — launch rule

Initial launch economics are intentionally independent of recovered cash:

1. **Portfolio baseline fee — fixed.**
2. **Monitoring fee — fixed recurring, only after baseline value is proven.**
3. **Formal auditor — contracted separately by the customer.**
4. **Success/recovery fee — disabled for the initial offer.**

This avoids making the product dependent on unresolved jurisdictional rules, professional independence, audit-cost clauses or fee-sharing arrangements.

## First pricing experiments

These are test cells, not validated market prices:

### Cell A — 10-agreement proof baseline
- Price hypothesis: **US$2,500 fixed**.
- Intended buyer: TTO that wants to test the workflow on a small bounded set.
- Economic guard: below the public ~US$3,000 average cost of one traditional UC desk audit, while screening ten agreements at portfolio-triage depth rather than pretending to perform ten formal desk audits.

### Cell B — 25-agreement baseline
- Price hypothesis: **US$5,000 fixed**.
- Intended job: establish operative versions, evidence gaps, deterministic reconciliations where supported and a ranked escalation set.

### Cell C — 75-agreement baseline
- Price hypothesis: **US$10,000 fixed**.
- Requires measured evidence that human normalization/review effort does not scale linearly with agreement count.

### Cell D — 150–200 agreement baseline
- Price hypothesis: **US$15,000–US$25,000 fixed**.
- Must not be offered until the 100+ agreement synthetic/assisted throughput model demonstrates acceptable review effort and data-handling reliability.

The price per agreement is deliberately not described as a desk-audit substitute. RoyaltyRecover performs a portfolio screening/baseline layer and routes selected cases into deeper desk/formal audit work.

## Unit-economics hard gates

A paid baseline should not be repeated at the same price if either is true:
- measured direct delivery cost exceeds **40%** of fixed-fee revenue; or
- median human review/normalization effort scales so close to one-for-one with agreement count that the portfolio advantage disappears.

Target gross margin for the assisted baseline after the first learning pilots: **>=60% before central overhead**, as an internal operating target, not a market claim.

Instrumentation required per pilot:
- agreements received / accepted / blocked;
- agreements with complete operative version set;
- evidence rows/records processed;
- analyst minutes;
- reviewer minutes;
- normalization/data-cleaning minutes;
- partner/auditor cost, if any;
- fixed-fee revenue;
- time to first defensible candidate;
- time to completed portfolio baseline;
- candidate rate;
- no-current-recovery rate;
- needs-data/professional-review rate;
- false-positive count confirmed later;
- formal-audit routes;
- auditor acceptance/rejection reasons;
- verified incremental recovered cash, only after payment evidence.

## Commercial truth policy

Never promise a recovery rate or money amount before source-supported analysis.

Allowed promise:
**"We independently baseline your royalty portfolio against the operative agreements, statements, payments and available source data; show what is supported; identify what is missing; and route only evidence-ready exceptions for deeper review."**

Disallowed promises include:
- "We recover 10% of your royalties.";
- "Every positive variance is owed.";
- "This replaces the independent auditor required by your agreement.";
- any recovery guarantee derived from historical third-party audit results.

## Pilot-ready sales gate

The assisted offer becomes technically sellable when RoyaltyRecover can reproducibly execute:

agreement + amendments -> immutable source lineage -> reviewer-approved rules -> statements/payments/data -> deterministic persisted reconciliation -> fail-closed finding -> register -> reviewer route -> auditor handoff packet -> outcome ledger.

The code can satisfy this technical gate before market validation. **Commercial readiness still requires real buyer/partner validation and real-client execution; synthetic evidence cannot substitute for those external proofs.**
