# RoyaltyRecover — Gate Decision 03

Date: 2026-08-29

## Gate 1 — Problem Validated

**PASS.**

Quantified university and commercial audit evidence demonstrates recurring material royalty underpayments, recoveries, audit costs and economically useful contractual audit rights.

## Gate 2 — Initial Vertical / Beachhead Selected

**PASS for initial go-to-market beachhead: university and research technology-transfer offices managing patent / technology running-royalty portfolios.**

This is a beachhead decision, not a permanent product restriction.

### Why this vertical now passes

Evidence is unusually dense and coherent:
- royalty-bearing patent/technology licensing is core operating activity;
- university systems publicly document risk-based royalty-audit workflows;
- University of Kentucky disclosed 170+ active license agreements and a procured specialist audit engagement;
- Kentucky disclosed one audit producing $2,068,138 additional royalty from a $31,765.84 audit cost;
- University of California reports systematic-monitoring resource constraints and supports centralized monitoring;
- UC benchmarks desk audits around $3,000 and full audits around $40,000–$60,000;
- UC states approximately 50% of desk audits, based on outside-consultant experience, identify concerns meriting transition to full audit;
- AUTM provides a concentrated buyer/professional ecosystem and documents material agreement/amendment activity.

The economic job is therefore concrete:

**screen a portfolio at a much lower cost per licensee than manual desk review, identify which licensees deserve professional desk/full audit attention, and prevent closing contractual windows from being missed.**

## Gate 3 — Wedge Frozen

**OPEN / NOT PASSED.**

The workflow itself is not novel. Connor and other audit/compliance providers already perform risk assessment and desk-review programs; enterprise royalty software and Quartiq overlap with continuous compliance. RoyaltyRecover must prove a superior operating wedge rather than claim category novelty.

Current candidate wedge:

**ZERO-MIGRATION RECOVERABILITY TRIAGE FOR B2B RUNNING-ROYALTY PORTFOLIOS**

Required differentiation proof:
1. materially lower portfolio screening cost per licensee than the approximately-$3k professional desk-audit benchmark;
2. minimal migration/integration burden;
3. deterministic source lineage and explicit no-recovery outcomes;
4. version/input staleness protection;
5. deadline-aware portfolio prioritization;
6. clean qualified-auditor/counsel handoff;
7. recurring re-screening without repeating a manual desk audit from scratch.
