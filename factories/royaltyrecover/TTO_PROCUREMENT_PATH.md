# RoyaltyRecover — TTO Procurement Path Evidence

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: MATERIAL GTM EVIDENCE — INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC, NOT UNIVERSAL

## University of Kentucky

UK's Business Procedures Manual classifies consulting/professional-skill engagements as Personal Service Contracts (PSCs).

Current UK policy states:
- PSCs of **US$10,000 or less** use the lower-value PSC procedure and are reported through Procurement Services to the Government Contract Review Committee before effective date;
- PSCs **over US$10,000** require an RFP / competitive solicitation unless an approved exception applies;
- outside consultants/professional services are included in the PSC category.

Sources:
- https://ufs.uky.edu/bpm/b-4-personal-service-contracts
- https://ufs.uky.edu/bpm/b-5-non-competitive-negotiations-singlesole-source-purchases
- https://purchasing.uky.edu/campus-departments-frequently-asked-questions

### Commercial implication

For a UK-like public university procurement regime, a **US$5,000 bounded proof baseline** is materially easier to test than an initial engagement above US$10,000. This does not mean procurement is instant or delegated to the TTO: formal university processing still applies.

The current Cell B price experiment (US$5,000 / 25 agreements) is therefore strategically useful as a procurement-friction experiment, not merely a pricing experiment.

## University of California

UC's January 2026 BUS-43 procurement policy states that buyers may use negotiation for purchases where annual expenditure with a supplier is below **US$100,000**, while purchases at or above US$100,000 are generally competitively bid unless an exception applies.

UC Small Business First additionally applies to many non-construction procurements between **US$10,000 and US$250,000** where feasible. Its public supplier guidance describes a streamlined quote-to-award process and states that one quote is required below US$100,000 within the covered program.

Sources:
- https://policy.ucop.edu/doc/3220485/bfb-bus-43
- https://procurement.ucop.edu/about-us/programs/small-business-first-program-uc-suppliers

### Commercial implication

The threshold structure is different from Kentucky and cannot be generalized campus-to-campus. However, a sub-US$10,000 first engagement again has a plausible friction advantage because it stays below the Small Business First range while remaining far below UC's US$100,000 general competitive-bid threshold.

This is an inference from published policy, not proof that a specific UC TTO can directly buy RoyaltyRecover at that price.

## RIT / federal-funds signal

Rochester Institute of Technology increased its micro-purchase threshold to **US$15,000 effective July 1, 2026**, including federally funded purchases under its stated procedures.

Source:
- https://www.rit.edu/procurement/updates-tips
- https://www.rit.edu/procurement/federal-funds-procedures

This reinforces that lower-dollar institutional entry offers can face materially lighter procurement processes, while also showing that thresholds differ by institution and funding source.

## Procurement-first GTM decision

Initial TTO sales motion should not begin with a US$15k–25k portfolio engagement.

Preferred sequence:

1. **US$2,500 / 10-agreement proof baseline** where permitted.
2. Measure delivery effort, buyer approval path, security review and usefulness.
3. **US$5,000 / 25-agreement baseline** if the first cell is too small to demonstrate portfolio value.
4. Expand to larger fixed-fee portfolio baselines only after the institution's procurement path is known.

The entry price must never be split artificially to evade procurement rules. Scope must be genuinely bounded, separately valuable and truthfully contracted.

## Buyer/process discovery fields

Every prospect discovery record should capture:
- institution type: public/private/nonprofit;
- contracting entity: university, research foundation, affiliate or other;
- buyer/champion role;
- budget owner;
- professional-services classification;
- procurement threshold relevant to the actual funding source;
- RFP/competitive quote requirement;
- small-business preference/certification rule;
- vendor registration requirement;
- information-security/privacy review requirement;
- signature authority;
- expected contracting lead time;
- whether the TTO can run a low-value proof without a full RFP;
- whether an incumbent royalty auditor/framework agreement already exists.

## Gate impact

Gate 3 improves because a low-dollar proof can plausibly avoid the heaviest competitive procurement in multiple evidenced institutions.

Gate 3 remains OPEN because published thresholds do not prove:
- TTO budget availability;
- buyer willingness;
- security review duration;
- whether RoyaltyRecover will be classified as consulting, software, audit-related professional services or another category;
- whether incumbent framework agreements block or simplify entry.
