## What remains unknown before outreach

For every candidate, independently verify before any message is sent:

- current official company website/contact path;
- appropriate commercial/estimating/procurement decision maker;
- whether opportunity discovery is performed internally, outsourced, relationship-led or portal-led;
- actual frequency of tender review;
- whether missed/irrelevant opportunities create measurable cost;
- whether a shortlist tool duplicates an incumbent workflow;
- lawful/appropriate outreach route.

Do not infer these facts from contract awards alone.

## Experiment design

The next commercial test should change the qualification variable while keeping the core value hypothesis bounded:

**ICP:** contractors with demonstrated recurring public-procurement participation.

**Problem question:** how much time is spent discovering and triaging tenders before deciding whether to bid?

**Assisted offer:** configure the company's real capability profile and deliver a small explainable shortlist of current public-work opportunities, then observe whether at least one item is judged actionable enough to save, investigate or assign.

**Primary learning metrics:**

1. reply rate;
2. explicit confirmation/denial of recurring tender-discovery pain;
3. current workaround;
4. time burden;
5. willingness to test;
6. first useful shortlist;
7. action on surfaced opportunity;
8. willingness to continue;
9. willingness to pay.
