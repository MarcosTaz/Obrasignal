`RULE REF != RULE APPLICABILITY`.

`APPROVED != RECOVERED`.

`SYMBOLIC EVIDENCE REF != VERIFIED OUTCOME EVIDENCE`.

`RECOVERED != FEE-BILLABLE WITHOUT CUSTOMER CONTRACT BASIS`.
