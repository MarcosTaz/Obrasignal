# Cloud Switching — Commercial Hypotheses v0.1

Status: **HYPOTHESIS ONLY** until T1 technical gate is exact-HEAD GREEN and real external demand evidence exists.

## Founding offer

Working offer: **Verified Migration Economics Review**.

The assisted lifecycle is intentionally split so the original business case is frozen before it can be rewritten retrospectively:

1. **T0 Baseline Certification** — inventory readiness, current-state cost baseline, compared alternatives, approved vs received incentives, forecasted switching cost/duration/downtime/run-rate, methodology version.
2. **Migration checkpoint** — optional wave/dual-run/decommission review during execution.
3. **Outcome Verification** — actual cost/duration/downtime/run-rate/incentives, comparability gate, variance and independent evidence grade.

The product must be able to conclude `DO_NOT_SWITCH_NOW`, `RENEW_TEMPORARILY`, or `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE`; it must never be commercially optimized to force a switch decision.

## Initial ICP hypothesis

Primary cohort: European mid-market / enterprise organizations planning or executing a VMware/infrastructure exit or major replatforming.

Entry roles, in order of expected practical access:

- FinOps / IT financial management lead
- CTO / CIO / infrastructure leader
- cloud/platform lead
- Finance as economic validator

Migration providers and cloud vendors may supply evidence, but cannot independently grade their own outcome.

## Qualification score v0.1

This is an operational hypothesis, not a validated conversion model.

- renewal/decision within 12 months: 20
- material estate (initial proxy >=100 VMs or equivalent economic footprint): 15
- at least two credible alternatives under consideration: 15
- original business case / forecast exists: 15
- billing baseline available: 10
- telemetry / estate evidence available: 10
- buyer willing to freeze forecast before decision: 10
- FinOps / Finance participates: 5

Tier A: 70–100. Tier B: 50–69. Below 50: do not spend assisted founder-time unless new evidence changes the score.

## Pricing hypotheses

No value below is validated willingness-to-pay.

- Baseline Certification: **EUR 1,500**
- Optional checkpoint: **EUR 1,000**
- Outcome Verification: **EUR 3,500**
- Typical lifecycle hypothesis: **EUR 5,000–6,000**

Provider incentive/funding is never treated as our revenue and never described as customer savings.

## First-revenue experiment

Goal: test whether organizations will pay to freeze and independently reconcile migration economics before the outcome is known.

Minimum external evidence target:

- 100 outreach-qualified prospects identified by real migration triggers
- 40 highly contextualized outreach attempts, only after explicit authorization to send
- 8 discovery conversations as a planning hypothesis, not a promised conversion
- 2 paid founding pilots as the first meaningful WTP signal

No free pilot should be counted as willingness-to-pay validation.

## Kill / downgrade gates

Downgrade the large-company thesis if any of these persist after real exposure:

1. Buyers refuse to freeze the original business case before migration.
2. Required evidence cannot be acquired without highly bespoke consulting effort.
3. Human hours per verified outcome remain approximately linear after 20–30 outcomes.
4. Customers value only one-off reconciliation and show no recurring demand for benchmark / forecast-error intelligence.
5. Independent evidence cannot be obtained without relying on the migration executor/provider self-attestation.
6. Cross-provider outcomes cannot be normalized into sufficiently comparable cohorts.

If (1) or (5) fails structurally, the strongest moat — independently evidenced `PROMISE → ACTUAL → VARIANCE` — is materially weakened.

## Recurring-product hypothesis

Transaction assurance is the acquisition/data-production engine, not the desired terminal business model.

Potential recurring product, still unvalidated:

- cohort benchmarks by route/workload/strategy
- forecast-error distributions for cost, duration, savings and payback
- evidence-quality filtered benchmark access
- promise-accuracy intelligence by route/strategy/executor only when cohort size, privacy and legal constraints permit

Until customers pay repeatedly for this intelligence, it must not be called a proven SaaS/network-effect business.
