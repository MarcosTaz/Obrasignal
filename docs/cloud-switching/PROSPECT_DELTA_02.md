## Updated trigger lesson

The acquisition engine should distinguish three different observable events:

1. **Renewal clock** — proves timing and incumbent spend, but may be closed to alternatives.
2. **Market dialogue / equivalent solutions** — proves timing plus challengeability and is much closer to the desired pre-decision wedge.
3. **Mixed-platform procurement** — proves heterogeneity but not necessarily migration; requires workload-level linkage before scoring alternatives.

Therefore, priority discovery should search market-dialogue/RFI language such as `or equivalent`, `similar licences`, `virtualization alternatives`, `replacement platform`, and `successor solution`, not merely the word `VMware`.
