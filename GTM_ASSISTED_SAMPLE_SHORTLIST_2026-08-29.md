## Sample usefulness protocol

When shown to a real prospect, ask them to classify each opportunity:

- `IRRELEVANT`
- `WORTH_OPENING`
- `WORTH_INVESTIGATING`
- `LIKELY_BID`
- `ALREADY_KNOWN`
- `STALE_OR_INVALID`

Then ask **why**. The reason is more useful than a raw click.

Capture:
- whether they already knew it;
- which field made it relevant/irrelevant;
- what information is missing for a bid/no-bid decision;
- whether receiving this shortlist earlier/repeatedly would change actual work;
- how long their current discovery/triage process takes.

## Truth constraints

- A current public notice is not automatically a qualified customer opportunity.
- A good profile match is not a recommendation to bid.
- A customer opening a notice is not first value by itself.
- A public tender discovered manually for this sample must not be reported as “found by ObraSignal automation.”
- Deadlines and terms must be rechecked at the primary source immediately before customer use because notices can be altered.
