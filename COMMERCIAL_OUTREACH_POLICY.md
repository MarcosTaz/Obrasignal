# ObraSignal Commercial Outreach Policy

Status: canonical commercial policy.

## Brand invariant

The product and company-facing brand is always written exactly as **ObraSignal**.

- Never translate, transliterate, localize, pluralize, or otherwise adapt the name `ObraSignal`.
- This applies to subjects, sender names, email bodies, signatures, landing pages, proposals, presentations, ads, partner material, filenames, metadata, and CRM fields.
- The domain, logo wording, and product name remain invariant in every market.
- Localize only the surrounding commercial language, terminology, CTA, salutation, examples, and procurement vocabulary.
- Localization must read naturally for the target market; it must not be a literal word-for-word translation where local business language differs.

## Contact quality

- Prefer a named relevant decision-maker and verified business address where available.
- Preserve the recipient company's legal/trading name exactly; do not translate company names.
- Use the recipient's local business language where confidently known; otherwise use the market's standard business language or English when more appropriate.
- Adapt public-procurement terminology to the country's normal usage.
- Do not invent a recipient name, role, company fact, contract history, customer claim, or product result.
- Keep each message commercially useful even if the recipient does not reply: explain the problem ObraSignal solves, the concrete value, and one low-friction next action.

## Sequence gate

A lower-priority country may be used earlier only when there is a concrete higher-value inbound lead, partner opportunity, or other explicit commercial reason. Ordinary list-building and outbound execution follow the order above.
