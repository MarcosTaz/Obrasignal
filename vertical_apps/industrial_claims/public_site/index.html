The problem

Suppliers can absorb customer-change costs because proof and economic status are scattered.

The commercial basis may sit in a PO, the instruction in another system, engineering hours in a ledger and prior credits elsewhere. Certactum reconstructs the chain while preserving the distinction between documented cost, evidence-supported value, claimed value, approved value and settled value.

Initial wedge

Start with customer-directed engineering and change cost.

The first Certactum case type is NRE / change-driven incremental cost for industrial suppliers. Direct adjacencies are considered only when they belong to the same customer, program and change mechanism.

Strong starting signals

  • A customer instruction, revision, cancellation or scope change can be identified.
  • There is a pre-change commercial or technical baseline.
  • Incremental engineering, tooling, material, freight or disruption cost can be documented.
  • Prior claim, approval, payment and mitigation can be reconciled.

Cases requiring more evidence

  • A cost exists, but no customer-caused event can yet be supported.
  • A spreadsheet estimate lacks its underlying documentary trail.
  • The amount has not reconciled reuse, prior recovery, approval or settlement.
  • Material contractual questions still require specialist review.
Certactum truth: cost is not automatically money owed. Evidence support, claim submission, approval and settlement are separate states. Every conclusion remains tied to its underlying evidence, chronology, calculation lineage and human review. When the chain is incomplete, the disposition is NO CLAIM / NEEDS REVIEW rather than an inflated recovery number. The dossier also states what still requires review and what the available evidence does not support.
