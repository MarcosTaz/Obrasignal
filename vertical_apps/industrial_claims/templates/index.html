Truth rules

A reference without securely captured documentary content is REFERENCE_ONLY and cannot become review-ready. Currency is explicit and immutable across reviewed economics and recovery recording. A recovered/closed outcome requires documentary evidence and is a final cumulative outcome that closes the finding, not an installment stream. Notice deadline values are declared for human review rather than independently verified by the software. An unknown notice deadline is evidence missing, not an infinite deadline; an expired declared date is an urgency signal requiring human review of waiver/exception possibilities, not proof of non-recoverability. READY_FOR_REVIEW means documented candidate ready for human review. APPROVED means approved for internal workflow only. Neither state is a legal conclusion, established recoverability, customer-contact authorization, external claim authority, or guaranteed recovery. Pricing remains a hypothesis until real market evidence exists.
