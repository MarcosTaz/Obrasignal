Truth rules

A reference without documentary content is REFERENCE_ONLY and cannot become review-ready. Currency is explicit and immutable across reviewed economics and recovery recording. A recovered/closed outcome requires hashed documentary evidence. An unknown notice deadline is evidence missing, not an infinite deadline. READY_FOR_REVIEW means documented candidate ready for human review; it is not a legal conclusion, established recoverability, or guaranteed recovery.
