Truth rules

A reference without securely captured documentary content remains REFERENCE_ONLY. Currency is explicit and immutable across reviewed economics and recovery recording. A recovered outcome requires documentary evidence and a final cumulative record. Notice deadline values are declared for human review rather than independently inferred. READY_FOR_REVIEW means documented candidate ready for human review. APPROVED records the independent internal decision. The Proof Pack preserves evidence state, calculation lineage, review history and documented outcome separately.
