## 1. First-pilot ICP gate

Prioritize a UK specialist contractor/subcontractor only when the proposed historical-closeout sample has:

- completed or late-stage projects with identifiable customer/project/contract scope;
- documentary chain covering at least certification/invoice/payment or retention/final-account state;
- one bounded historical population that can be manually audited end-to-end;
- finance/commercial reviewer access;
- exports/documents available without broad production credentials;
- one currency per reconciliation scope unless an explicit reviewed FX model is introduced;
- suspected closeout cash exposure that can be tested from records without first deciding a legal dispute.

Hold as `NOT_QUALIFIED` or `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` when the opportunity depends principally on unresolved variation/time/LD entitlement, narrative estimates, missing project identity, unbounded mixed-currency aggregation, automatic debtor contact, or a legal/regulated collection act.

### Qualification score

UNKNOWN scores zero. Never infer a positive answer.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project/contract identity | absent | partial | consistently linked |
| Certification/invoice evidence | absent | partial | usable source chain |
| Payment/cash evidence availability | absent | ledger only | independently verifiable evidence available |
| Retention/final-account evidence | absent | partial | explicit documentary basis |
| Due-date truth | narrative | incomplete | evidence-backed dates available |
| Human reviewer | absent | unconfirmed | named internal reviewer role available |
| Dataset boundary | unbounded | partially bounded | auditable pilot population |
| Legal/perimeter fit | requires legal/collection act | uncertain | analytics/evidence prep only |

- `0–6`: `NOT_QUALIFIED`
- `7–11`: `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`
- `12–16`: `ASSISTED_PILOT_CANDIDATE`

This score proves pilot admissibility only, never recoverability.

## 3. Value-state truth

Never collapse these states:

- `QUEUE_VALUE`: evidence-supported amount for review/action; not recovered cash;
- `ACTION_APPROVED_VALUE`: customer approved an action; still not cash;
- `RECOVERY_REQUESTED_VALUE`: request/action occurred; still not cash;
- `SETTLED_VALUE`: settlement evidence exists; still distinct from received cash;
- `VERIFIED_CASH`: independently verified exact-scope bank receipt passed product verification;
- `ATTRIBUTED_VERIFIED_CASH`: verified cash is independently attributable to the EarnedCash-assisted case/action;
- `FEE_DUE`: commercial agreement plus verified attribution support a fee;
- `PLATFORM_REVENUE_PAID`: separate settlement evidence proves our fee was paid.

A positive queue can terminate at verified cash `0`; that is a valid pilot outcome.

## 4. Initial offer

Working offer: `Historical Closeout Cash Audit — Assisted Pilot`.

Bounded promise:

- reconcile a limited historical population of completed/late-stage projects;
- identify document-supported closeout items requiring review/action;
- separate due, not-yet-due, disputed and unknown value;
- provide evidence lineage and customer-controlled next action;
- verify cash only from independently verifiable receipt evidence;
- measure delivery effort and outcome.

Explicit non-promises:

- guaranteed recovery;
- legal advice or representation;
- automatic debtor contact;
- debt collection;
- lending/factoring;
- extrapolated recovery percentage across total revenue/spend;
- self-certified cash recovery.

## 5. Reversible exposure message skeleton

`We run a bounded historical closeout cash audit for specialist contractors. Instead of treating the aged ledger as proof of recoverability, we reconcile completed/late-stage projects across certification, invoicing, payment, retention and final-account evidence. Missing or disputed facts remain UNKNOWN; the first pilot can be limited to a small historical project population and requires no production-system write access or automatic customer contact.`

`The output is a human-reviewable closeout queue with evidence lineage, plus verified cash tracking only if independently verifiable receipt evidence later exists.`

This is preparation copy, not authorized external communication.
