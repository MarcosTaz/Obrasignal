## Ranking rule

Priority = customer abandonment risk × value blocked / truth risk. Privacy/tenant leakage outranks commercial or cosmetic friction.

## Truth boundary

A resolved engine assertion does not prove real customer understanding, willingness to pay, retention, or real economic outcome. Synthetic journey gates only prove deterministic product behavior against an independent oracle. Integration GREEN is not canonical release readiness. `MULTI_USER = NOT_MODELED`, password recovery, Web checkout and Notification lifecycle remain owner gaps; a Customer Journey candidate must not manufacture those capabilities.
